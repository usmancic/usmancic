### 🔬 Research areas

- **Hate speech and offensive language:** LLM-based and translation-based multilingual detection (English, Spanish, Urdu), bias-aware QLoRA-tuned LLaMA models, Urdu YouTube discourse
- **Health NLP on social media:** explainable opioid-overdose detection, LLMs for drug-use and overdose symptoms, opioid named-entity recognition, the SABIA detection tool
- **Efficient and trustworthy models:** distilling a 7B LLM into a 125M encoder (52× faster, 56× less energy), calibration, model cascades, explainable AI
- **Medical and visual AI:** vision transformers for chest X-rays and plant disease, breast-cancer survival prediction with ML and GPT-4
- **Applied NLP:** RoBERTa bug detection in app reviews, AI-generated text detection, computational pragmatics
